## 3. Quora

Question: "I cannot upload my photo on neet application form. What should I do?"
URL: https://www.quora.com/I-cannot-upload-my-photo-on-neet-application-form-What-should-I-do
(Found via search on 2026-10-31; question date not confirmed. Check it's still open and recent before answering.)

Draft answer:

If the portal refuses the upload with an error, the problem is almost always the file, not your face. Check three things against the bulletin.

First, format. NEET UG 2026 asked for the photo in JPG/JPEG. iPhone photos are often HEIC and website images are often WebP, so convert to JPG first.

Second, file size. The NEET UG 2026 range was 10–200 KB. People usually check only the maximum, but a heavily cropped photo can fall under 10 KB and get refused too.

Third, the photo rules, which can get the form rejected later even if the upload works: white background, your face covering 80% of the image with ears visible, no mask, a recent photo (the 2026 bulletin wanted one taken on or after 1 January 2026), and no computer-made or edited photos. A live photo is also captured and matched with your Aadhaar photo.

Use the original camera file, not a WhatsApp-forwarded copy, crop so your face fills the frame, and zoom into the preview before you submit. I wrote up every common cause with fixes here: https://documently.in/guides/why-online-form-photo-gets-rejected/
